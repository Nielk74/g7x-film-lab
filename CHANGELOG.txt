Project modifications by G7X Film Lab contributors:
- Bake the Portra 400, Gold 200, Ektar 100, Pro 400H and Tri-X 400 profiles
  into fixed, pointwise sRGB-to-sRGB color and tone transforms.
- Use fixed 5500 K virtual scene light and fixed Digital Reference / Graded
  Print. Preserve local cross-layer color interactions; disable spatial
  diffusion, grain, halation, lens flare, adjacency and scene auto-metering.
- Add an explicit display-input adaptation calibrated against each stock's
  extended model gray ramp. Its authored finishing settings are recorded per
  look; these are not additional manufacturer measurements.
- Export 65-cubed desktop CUBE LUTs and independently authored Canon PF3 files.
- Include original format tooling, numerical checks, source provenance and
  photo examples. Physical film/camera matching is not established by these
  software checks. No Canon firmware or Canon software is redistributed.
